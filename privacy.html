Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

UMBRAL Publisher is used only by the UMBRAL channel owner to publish the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about the public or any other person.

What it stores. After the owner authorizes the app with TikTok, an access token for the owner's own TikTok account is kept in a private file on the owner's computer. It is used only to upload videos. Nothing is sent to any third party other than TikTok.

Deleting data. The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites, and can delete the stored token file.
